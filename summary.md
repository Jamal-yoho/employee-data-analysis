# Employee Data Analysis – Project Report

## 1. Problem Statement
In this project, I worked on employee data to understand the number of employees in each department, their age groups, salary details and attrition.

## 2. Dataset
I used an employee dataset with 1,470 records and 35 columns. It contains details such as department, age, monthly income, attrition and years at the company.

## 3. Key Findings
The bar chart shows the number of employees working in each department.
The histogram helps to understand the age distribution of employees.
The box plot shows the differences in monthly income among employees.

## 4. Recommendations
The company can review employee numbers in each department and plan hiring based on its requirements.
The company can review salary differences to make sure employees are paid fairly.

## 5. Limitations
The analysis is based only on the available employee dataset.
The dataset may not include all the reasons.
The analysis shows patterns in the available data, but it cannot predict future employee behaviour.
